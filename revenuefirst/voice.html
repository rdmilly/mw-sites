Frequently Asked Questions

Does it actually sound like a real person?
Yes. Our AI uses natural voice synthesis with sub-second response times. It understands trade terminology, handles interruptions, and callers regularly think they're speaking with a real receptionist.
What if a customer needs to speak to me directly?
The AI can transfer urgent calls directly to your phone or any team member. You set the rules — emergencies go straight to you, everything else gets booked automatically.
Do I need to change my phone number?
No. You keep your existing business number. We set up simple call forwarding — when you can't answer, calls automatically route to your AI receptionist. Takes 2 minutes.
What happens after the free trial?
If you love it, your plan continues at the monthly rate. If not, cancel with one click — no contracts, no fees, no hassle.
Can it handle multiple calls at once?
Yes! Unlike a human receptionist, your AI handles unlimited simultaneous calls. Every caller gets answered immediately — no hold music, no busy signals.
